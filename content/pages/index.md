# AI runs on open infrastructure

<p class="rai-lede">The ASF launched the Responsible AI initiative to strengthen the open source
technologies that underpin modern artificial intelligence, and to help Apache communities adopt
AI in a way that is open, transparent, and aligned with the Foundation's values.</p>

<p class="rai-actions">
<a class="rai-btn rai-btn-primary" href="get-involved.html">Get involved</a>
<a class="rai-btn" href="https://news.apache.org/foundation/entry/the-apache-software-foundation-launches-10m-responsible-ai-initiative-with-initial-1-75m-donation">Read the announcement</a>
</p>

</div>

AI systems worldwide depend on critical Apache projects. The initiative works to ensure that the
ASF projects embedded in AI systems have expanded access to the models and resources they need to
remain secure, transparent, and governed in the public interest.

<div class="rai-cards">

<div class="rai-card">

## Leveraging

Expanded access to AI models, tooling, and resources for the Apache projects already embedded in
the AI ecosystem.

</div>

<div class="rai-card">

## Innovating

Foundation-led programs and emerging AI projects growing under open, community governance and the
Apache License 2.0.

</div>

<div class="rai-card">

## Educating

Shared guidance on licensing, provenance, security, and attribution for communities adopting AI.
