## What is the Responsible AI initiative?

An Apache Software Foundation effort to strengthen the open source technologies that underpin
modern AI and to help ASF communities adopt AI responsibly. It provides access to AI models and
tooling, ecosystem support for AI-focused projects, and a place to share good practices. See
[About](about.html).

## Who leads it?

A **Vice President, Responsible AI**, appointed by the ASF Board. The work itself is open to
contributors from across the Foundation and the wider community. See [About](about.html) for the
current officeholder.

## Is the initiative's guidance binding on my project?

No. The initiative offers guidance, resources, and shared good practices. Apache projects remain
self-governing and adopt what is useful to them.

## What principles guide the responsible use of AI at the ASF?

Four core principles — human oversight, licensing integrity, security, and documentation — rooted
in the ASF philosophy of *community over code*. They are set out in full on
[What we do](what-we-do.html#guiding-principles).

## How can I get involved in a specific area?

Volunteers organize around four work streams. Pick one from
[What we do](what-we-do.html#work-streams) and reply on the mailing list to join it.

## How can I take part or ask a question?

Through the mailing list or the ASF Slack channel — both are listed on
[Get involved](get-involved.html).

## How can I support the initiative?

See [Support the initiative](https://apache.org/foundation/initiatives/responsibleai) on
apache.org.
