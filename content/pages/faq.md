## What is the Responsible AI initiative?

It is an Apache Software Foundation effort to strengthen the open source technologies
that underpin modern AI and to help ASF communities adopt AI responsibly. It provides
access to AI models and tooling, ecosystem support for AI-focused projects, and a place
to share good practices.

## Who leads it?

A **Vice President, Responsible AI**, appointed by the ASF Board, leads the initiative.
The work itself is open to contributors from across the Foundation and the wider
community.

## Is the initiative's guidance binding on my project?

The initiative offers guidance, resources, and shared good practices. Apache projects
remain self-governing and adopt what is useful to them.

## What principles guide the responsible use of AI at the ASF?

Four core principles: **human oversight, licensing integrity, security, and
documentation** — rooted in the ASF philosophy of *community over code*.

## How can I get involved in a specific area (security, innovation, policy, education)?

Volunteers currently organize around four work streams — **Security**, **Innovation**,
**Policy**, and **Education, Adoption & Outreach**. Reply on the mailing list to join one —
see [Get Involved](get-involved.html) for details.

## How can I take part or ask a question?

Join **discuss@rai.apache.org**, open to ASF Members, or ask to be invited to the
**#ai-discuss** channel on the ASF Slack. See [Get Involved](get-involved.html) for details.

*This FAQ will grow as questions come up on the mailing list.*
