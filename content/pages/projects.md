Dozens of [ASF projects](https://projects.apache.org/projects.html?category) are
integral to a rapidly-evolving AI ecosystem. Developers rely upon
highly-performant Apache infrastructure for AI solutions, including scalable
machine learning, high-volume data storage and real-time distributed databases,
analytics, natural language processors (NLPs), graph systems, and so much more.

Apache Projects and services under the Responsible AI Initiative include:

- [llmao](https://llm.apache.org): free multi-model access for ASF Projects and
  their communities (ASF credentials required)
- [Gofannon](https://github.com/the-ai-alliance/gofannon): agent workbench at
  the [AI Alliance](https://thealliance.ai)
- [Magpie](https://magpie.apache.org): AI-assisted infrastructure and framework
  for managing Open Source software repositories
- [Sourcelume](https://sourcelume.apache.org): AI model training data provenance
  tracking and verification tool
- [ASF Project Security Scanning & Triage
  program](https://news.apache.org/foundation/entry/security-scanning-at-foundation-scale):
  advanced, AI-powered large-scale security scans initially run across dozens of
  ASF Projects and hundreds of repositories

The ASF Responsible AI Initiative works closely with Apache PMCs (Project
Management Committees), as well as ASF
[Infrastructure](https://infra.apache.org),
[Security](https://security.apache.org), and
[Tooling](https://tooling.apache.org) Initiative teams.
