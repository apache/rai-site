Everyone interested in the responsible use of AI at the ASF is welcome to follow the work and take
part. The initiative follows the Apache Way, and its discussion list is open to ASF Members.

## Where the work happens

| Channel | Details |
| --- | --- |
| Mailing list | **discuss@rai.apache.org**, open to ASF Members. Subscribe by emailing **discuss-subscribe@rai.apache.org**, or browse the [archives](https://lists.apache.org/list.html?discuss@rai.apache.org) |
| Chat | **#rai-discuss** on the [ASF Slack](https://infra.apache.org/slack.html) — ask to be invited |
| Source and issues | [apache/rai-site](https://github.com/apache/rai-site) |

Introduce yourself, share what your project is doing with AI, and tell us where expanded model
access or tooling would help. If you would like to join one of the four
[work streams](what-we-do.html#work-streams), reply on the mailing list and say which one.

## Contribute to this website

This site lives in the [apache/rai-site](https://github.com/apache/rai-site) repository. Pages are
written in **Markdown** under `content/pages/`, and the site is built with Pelican.

- For a small fix, edit a page directly on GitHub (the pencil icon) and open a pull request.
- For larger changes, open a branch or a GitHub issue to discuss first.

## Help build the initiative

The initiative brings together contributors from across the Foundation — Infrastructure, Security,
Tooling, the Incubator, and individual projects. If you would like to help with a specific area,
say hello on the mailing list and we will point you to where the work is happening.

You can also [support the initiative](https://apache.org/foundation/initiatives/responsibleai)
directly.

_More detailed contribution guidance will be added here as the initiative's processes take shape._
