Apache Responsible AI

The Apache Software Foundation (ASF) launched the Responsible AI initiative to strengthen the open source technologies that underpin modern artificial intelligence, and to help Apache communities adopt AI in a way that is open, transparent, and aligned with the ASF's values.

AI systems worldwide depend on critical Apache projects. The initiative works to ensure that the ASF projects embedded in AI systems have expanded access to the models and resources they need to remain secure, transparent, and governed in the public interest.

What guides us

The initiative is grounded in the ASF's guidelines for the responsible use of AI — human oversight, licensing integrity, security, and documentation — core principles rooted in the Foundation's longstanding philosophy of community over code.

Highlights

  • An open, Foundation-wide initiative to strengthen the Apache projects that underpin AI.
  • Led by a Vice President, Responsible AI, appointed by the ASF Board.
  • Coordinated on the discuss@rai.apache.org mailing list, open to ASF Members, and the #ai-discuss channel on the ASF Slack.
