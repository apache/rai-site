Projects

Dozens of ASF projects are integral to a rapidly-evolving AI ecosystem. Developers rely upon highly-performant Apache infrastructure for AI solutions, including scalable machine learning, high-volume data storage and real-time distributed databases, analytics, natural language processors (NLPs), graph systems, and so much more.

Apache Projects and services under the Responsible AI Initiative include:

  • llmao: free multi-model access for ASF Projects and their communities (ASF credentials required)
  • Gofannon: agent workbench at the AI Alliance
  • Magpie: AI-assisted infrastructure and framework for managing Open Source software repositories
  • Sourcelume: AI model training data provenance tracking and verification tool
  • ASF Project Security Scanning & Triage program: advanced, AI-powered large-scale security scans initially run across dozens of ASF Projects and hundreds of repositories

The ASF Responsible AI Initiative works closely with Apache PMCs (Project Management Committees), as well as ASF Infrastructure, Security, and Tooling Initiative teams.
